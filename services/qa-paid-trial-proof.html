What a first paid trial includes

Scope

One flow, one feature area, or one bug cluster. Good examples: signup, checkout sandbox, onboarding, settings, upload, search, chatbot, or mobile first-run.

Evidence

Short repro notes, expected versus actual behavior, severity, environment, and screenshots or public links where appropriate.

Output

A compact issue table, risk summary, recommended fixes, and a regression checklist that the team can act on immediately.

Boundary

Staging access, test accounts, dummy data, public repositories, or minimal reproductions are enough. Production secrets and private customer data are out of scope.

Deliverable shape

Part What you get Why it helps
Issue table Each finding has severity, area, repro steps, expected behavior, actual behavior, and suggested next action. It lets a founder or engineering lead decide what to fix first without reading a long report.
Risk summary A short view of what can block launch, harm conversion, confuse users, or create support load. It separates launch blockers from polish items.
Regression checklist A short checklist for retesting after fixes, written in plain product language. It reduces the chance of fixing one bug while reintroducing another.
Next slice A practical recommendation for whether to continue with deeper QA, automation, product review, or stop. It keeps the first milestone small and prevents scope drift.

Good fit

Before launch

You need a fresh pass on one risky flow before a release, demo, or customer handoff.

Bug-heavy beta

You have rough user feedback and need it sorted into reproducible issues and priorities.

Small founder team

You do not need a full QA department. You need a focused outside pass with clear notes.

Automation later

You want to know what is worth automating after the first manual pass, not before.

Not a fit

  • Tasks that require production passwords, private customer records, real payment credentials, bank data, wallet data, KYC details, production cookies, or production tokens.
  • Work that asks for CAPTCHA bypass, anti-bot bypass, scraping private dashboards, account automation, refund handling, dispute handling, or platform rule bypass.
  • Open-ended product management, full QA ownership, or broad consulting without a narrow first milestone.
